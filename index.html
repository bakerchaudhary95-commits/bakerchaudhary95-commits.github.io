How Etsy fees work

Etsy does not charge one fee. Each sale can carry up to four charges, and they stack. That is why a $30 sale can pay out closer to $26 before you count materials and postage.

Listing fee. $0.20 for each listing. You pay it again when the listing renews after four months and when a multi-quantity listing sells.

Transaction fee. 6.5% of the item price plus shipping and gift wrapping. Many new sellers miss that shipping is included.

Payment processing. In the US it is 3% plus $0.25 on the order total. Other countries have different rates, which is why you can edit them in the calculator.

Offsite Ads. Only when the buyer came through an Etsy ad on another site. It is 15% of the order (12% for shops over $10,000 a year), capped at $100 per order. Once your shop is above the threshold, the ads are required.

How to price so you still profit

Work backwards. Add your item cost, real postage and the profit you want, then add the fees. The tool above does this for you. If you offer free shipping, add the postage into the item price, because the 6.5% fee will apply to it either way.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Etsy take from each sale?

On a typical US sale Etsy charges a $0.20 listing fee, a 6.5% transaction fee and 3% + $0.25 payment processing. That is roughly 10% of the order total before any advertising fees.

Does the Etsy transaction fee apply to shipping?

Yes. The 6.5% transaction fee is charged on the item price plus the shipping and gift wrapping the buyer pays.

When is the Etsy listing fee charged again?

The $0.20 listing fee is charged when you publish a listing, when it renews after four months, and when a multi-quantity listing sells.

What are Etsy Offsite Ads fees?

Offsite Ads fees apply only to orders that come from an Etsy ad on another site. The rate is 15% of the order total, or 12% for shops with over $10,000 in yearly sales, capped at $100 per order.

Rates last checked September 2026. Etsy can change its fees at any time, so confirm on Etsy's own seller fee page before you set prices. Sales tax and VAT are not included. This site is not affiliated with Etsy.
